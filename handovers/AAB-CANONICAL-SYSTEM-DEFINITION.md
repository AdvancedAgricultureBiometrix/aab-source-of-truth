# AAB Canonical System Definition

**Status:** CURRENT AND AUTHORITATIVE  
**Established:** 30 August 2026 (AWST)  
**Applies to:** the identity, purpose, architecture, operating model and authority boundaries of Advanced Agriculture Biometrix (AAB)

## Authority of this record

This record defines what AAB is as a whole.

It supersedes any interpretation that reduces AAB to Country Discovery, an onboarding portal, a chatbot, a formulation tool, a dashboard or a conventional agritech application. Those are components or interfaces within a much larger governed system.

The [AAB Country Onboarding Path — Canonical Continuity Record](AAB-COUNTRY-ONBOARDING-CANONICAL-PATH.md) remains authoritative for onboarding sequence, rehearsal classification and country activation. Task-specific canonical records remain authoritative within their narrower scope. If two records appear to conflict, stop and report the conflict rather than silently redesigning the system.

## Canonical definition

> **AAB is a sovereign, governed scientific-intelligence and agricultural-development operating system that turns country problems and opportunities into evidence-linked, human-authorised research, candidate ingredients, formulations, trials, regulatory dossiers and controlled manufacturing transfer—while preserving provenance, uncertainty, contradictions, negative results and the separation of administrative, scientific, regulatory and production authority.**

AAB is designed to help countries, research institutions and authorised professionals investigate agricultural needs within sovereign data and governance boundaries. Its cognitive systems organise and reason over evidence; they do not replace human scientific or governance authority.

## The complete operating chain

```text
country problem or opportunity
→ governed evidence acquisition
→ provenance-linked cognitive relationships
→ hypothesis and knowledge-gap formation
→ authorised investigation
→ candidate ingredient
→ formulation development
→ controlled trial
→ observations and outcomes
→ contradictions, negative results and learning
→ regulatory translation and dossier
→ controlled manufacturing transfer
```

Country Discovery is the governed intelligence-acquisition and orientation layer at the beginning of this chain. Onboarding establishes who may enter each governed scope. Neither Discovery nor onboarding is the whole of AAB.

## Architecture is not the same as operational proof

A table, workflow or interface proves that a capability has been designed or built. It does not prove that the capability has been used successfully, authorised for production or populated with valid scientific records.

At the inspected checkpoint, many downstream operating objects were intentionally empty, including scientific memories, hypotheses, mechanism hypotheses, knowledge gaps, contradictions, negative learning, ingredients, formulation versions, trials, observations, outcomes, governance decisions, regulatory dossiers and manufacturing transfer packages.

That state is correct for the controlled build-and-validation stage. Future chats must always distinguish:

1. **architecturally present**;
2. **implemented and connected**;
3. **populated with controlled evidence**;
4. **successfully rehearsal-tested**;
5. **authorised for live operation**.

Never collapse these states into a single claim that a capability “exists” or “works.”

## Authority and sovereignty boundaries

- Authentication proves identity control; it does not create authority.
- Persisted protected records determine tenancy, membership, role, scope, routing and capability.
- The main AAB Supabase is the platform control plane.
- Each country Supabase is an isolated sovereign tenancy.
- A protected server-to-server bridge provisions authorised country records without consolidating databases or making the Platform Owner a country member.
- Administrative authority cannot become scientific approval.
- Cognitive brains and algorithms are advisory and governed; they cannot autonomously admit evidence, approve findings, create ingredient status, authorise trials, satisfy regulation or release manufacturing.
- Evidence remains provenance-linked, uncertainty-aware and quarantined until the applicable human-controlled gate is satisfied.
- Contradictions and negative results are preserved as learning, not hidden as failure.
- A rehearsal record can never satisfy a future government, production, legal, scientific, regulatory or external-invitation gate unless an expressly authorised production process creates the required separate record.

## What AAB is not

AAB is not:

- merely a Country Discovery website;
- merely an onboarding or identity service;
- an autonomous scientific decision-maker;
- a system that converts authentication into authority;
- a shared global database that erases country sovereignty;
- a route around research institutions, scientists, regulators or authorised governance;
- a system that treats absence of data as evidence of success; or
- a demonstration that may be represented as government or production approval.
