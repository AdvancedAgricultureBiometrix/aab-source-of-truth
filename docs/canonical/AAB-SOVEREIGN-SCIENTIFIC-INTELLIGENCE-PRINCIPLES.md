# AAB Sovereign Scientific Intelligence Principles

Status: Canonical architectural direction
Applies to: AAB country onboarding, discovery, scientific investigation, evidence memory and future inter-country exchange

## Purpose

AAB exists to preserve the parts of scientific work that conventional systems routinely lose: evidence provenance, reasoning chains, uncertainty, contradictions, negative results, unsuccessful trials, observations and the authority behind every consequential decision.

AAB is not a conventional global software-as-a-service database. Its operational model is sovereign-first: each onboarded country receives an isolated environment under its own governed authority. Global scientific learning may be supported later, but it must never require another system to query a country's operational database.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **One operational country, one isolated environment.** A live country tenancy must not share operational tables, private evidence or Brain memory with another country.
2. **Facts are not scenarios.** Source-backed observations, computed signals, hypotheses, scientific decisions and administrative decisions remain separately labelled.
3. **Uncertainty is preserved.** Missing evidence becomes a visible knowledge gap. Contradictory evidence and unsuccessful results are retained rather than silently reconciled or removed.
4. **Human scientific authority is preserved.** AAB may discover, compare, challenge and recommend. It cannot independently approve a candidate, create scientific consensus, activate an ingredient or authorise formulation or trial use.
5. **Administration is not scientific authority.** A Head Admin manages participation, institutions, legal conditions and workspace administration but cannot rewrite scientific evidence or decisions.
6. **Capability changes require progressive gates.** Recording an observation should not require national approval. Candidate classification, experimental ingredient projection, ingredient activation, Brain admission, formulation use, trial approval and external release are separate consequential operations.
7. **Provenance is permanent and attributable.** Scientific records are versioned and cryptographically hashed where appropriate. Corrections supersede earlier records; they do not silently erase them.
8. **No automatic cross-country learning.** Information leaves a sovereign environment only through an explicit, authorised export operation.
9. **Data minimisation precedes exchange.** Proposed external packages must be limited to the information required for the approved scientific purpose and reviewed for disclosure and re-identification risk.
10. **Future capabilities remain labelled as future.** A design intention must not be represented as an operational capability until it has been built, secured and validated.

## Four information tiers

### Tier 1 — Sovereign private

Raw datasets, researcher identities, exact coordinates, formulations, trial files, unpublished records, proprietary material and security-sensitive information remain within the country-controlled environment. The architecture prohibits external release except through an authorised export operation.

### Tier 2 — Domestic collaborative

Authorised domestic institutions may collaborate under country-defined roles, purpose limitations and sharing rules. Domestic access does not create permission for international release.

### Tier 3 — Federated scientific learning

A country may deliberately release a minimised scientific learning package after scientific, privacy and national-release review. Such a package may include an approved classification, method, negative result, contradiction, safety warning or reusable reasoning pattern without disclosing protected sources, locations, identities, formulations or commercial ownership.

### Tier 4 — Public knowledge

Officially published or explicitly released knowledge may be made publicly discoverable under its licence, attribution and permitted-use conditions.

## Separation of authority

- **Head Admin:** invitations, institutional onboarding, country terms and workspace administration.
- **Researchers:** evidence, observations and measurement capture within assigned scope.
- **Scientists:** scientific assessment, classification, contradictions and recommendations within assigned authority.
- **Data stewards:** privacy classification, minimisation and disclosure-risk review.
- **Institutional or national release authority:** approval of information leaving the sovereign environment.
- **AAB:** preservation, provenance, comparison, knowledge-gap detection and governed recommendations; never autonomous approval.

## Prohibited implementation drift

No AAB implementation may introduce cross-country operational queries, silent evidence overwrites, automatic scientific approval, automatic Brain admission, automatic ingredient activation, automatic formulation eligibility or an administrative ability to erase scientific history.
