## Candidate export package requirements

Every future export must include:

- issuing country and authorised releasing institution;
- export purpose and permitted use;
- information tier and sensitivity classification;
- scientific claim type and evidence state;
- uncertainty, contradictions and known limitations;
- provenance references or approved evidence snapshots;
- minimisation and re-identification review;
- licence, attribution and expiry or withdrawal conditions;
- scientific approval and national-release signatures;
- cryptographic integrity receipt;
- a statement of information deliberately withheld.

## Exchange design boundaries

- No direct connection to sovereign operational databases.
- No automatic export, ingestion or Brain admission.
- No requirement to reveal exact formulations, coordinates, identities or proprietary files.
- No conversion of an administrative release into scientific consensus.
- No deletion of a previously received version; correction occurs through a superseding signed record.
- A receiving country decides whether imported learning may enter its own governed scientific-review process.

## Proof gates before Phase 4

Federation must not begin until AAB has validated country isolation, domestic permissions, evidence provenance, negative-result preservation, disagreement handling, export minimisation, reviewer identity, revocation behaviour and independent security testing.
