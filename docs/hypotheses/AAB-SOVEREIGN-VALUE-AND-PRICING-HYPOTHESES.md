## Purpose

This document preserves early commercial reasoning without presenting it as established fact. Economic value and sovereign pricing must be supported by country-specific evidence, procurement research, cost modelling and buyer validation before external use.

## Potential value categories to test

- avoided duplication of unsuccessful research;
- retention of institutional knowledge when people or administrations change;
- earlier recognition of biosecurity, environmental or supply-chain signals;
- improved traceability of scientific decisions and national intellectual property;
- better use of existing research, observations and negative trial results;
- reduced coordination cost across authorised domestic institutions;
- controlled licensing or exchange of country-approved scientific learning.

## Required validation

- define the actual deployment, support and sovereign-infrastructure cost base;
- model value from defensible national datasets;
- distinguish avoided loss from realised cash savings;
- test procurement thresholds and buying authorities in target countries;
- assess comparable national research, data-sovereignty and decision-support programmes;
- test willingness to pay through confidential institutional discovery;
- define pilot, implementation, annual operation and optional capability pricing separately;
- obtain legal, tax, security and public-procurement advice.

## External wording until validated

AAB may be described as sovereign scientific infrastructure designed to preserve national scientific learning without requiring data colonisation. No quantified annual benefit, guaranteed saving, security guarantee or sovereign price should be stated without supporting evidence and approval.
