# AAB Country Discovery Engine

The Country Discovery Engine is the pre-AAB information-gathering layer used by an authorised head of state, government or platform owner. It is country-scoped, revisit-able and provenance-led.

## Output contract

Every scan groups results as candidates detected, new candidates requiring classification, known types not detected, or evidence coverage gaps. Signals below the minimum candidate threshold remain internally quarantined. “Not detected” is permitted only after adequate source coverage.

A displayed lead remains country-scoped, unverified, provenance-linked, safety-review required and ineligible for formulations, trials or automatic brain promotion. The engine may explain an investigation rationale; it must not present an assumed benefit.

## Country onboarding

Country configuration is data-driven: jurisdiction identity, authoritative boundaries, enabled source adapters, language, evidence policies and scan recipes. Candidate classes guide discovery but never limit it. Unfamiliar evidence may become a quarantined classification lead without modifying the global taxonomy.

Pitch environments use dated public-evidence snapshots and must name sources. They are not a substitute for live connectors. An onboarded country receives its own private database and governed source connections.

## Governed transition

Discovery workspace → public evidence → threshold and provenance checks → quarantined lead → local request download → individual upload → scientist, safety and governance review → limited AAB access only if approved.
