## 1. Purpose

Experience from people who have run agricultural and development programmes is valuable, and easily lost. It is also not research evidence. This register keeps such experience, and the strategic hypotheses it suggests, in one governed place:

1. so that it is not lost;
2. so that it is never presented as verified fact, AAB evidence or an AAB commitment;
3. so that each entry names what would need to be true, and what evidence could test it;
4. so that each entry is linked to the existing capability, contract gap or open decision it bears on, rather than to a new capability.

---

## 2. Classes of entry

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PRACTITIONER_TESTIMONY` | An observation reported by a person from their professional experience. It is recorded as they reported it. It has not been independently verified, and it is not AAB evidence. |
| `STRATEGIC_RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS` | A proposition about strategy, financing, institutions or development that would need evidence before anyone relied on it. |

Every entry carries the verification status `UNVERIFIED` until a recorded decision, citing evidence, changes it.

---

## 3. Rules

1. **Not evidence.** An entry is not a CAP-04 record, is not admitted, and is never cited as support, opposition or context in CAP-05, CAP-09 or any other capability. Evidence that tests an entry enters AAB only through the governed evidence path (CAP-02, CAP-03, CAP-04), on its own provenance.
2. **No causal claims.** An entry may state a reported or hypothesised relationship. It never establishes that one thing causes another (domain register, amendment of 2026-10-17).
3. **No recommendations, endorsements or commitments.** An entry never recommends an intervention, endorses a funder, describes a funding opportunity as available, or commits AAB to any financing, partnership or programme.
4. **Anonymised by default.** A testimony source is identified in this public repository only by a role description and a source code. The person's identity is held by the Platform Owner, outside the repository, and is published only with that person's recorded consent.
5. **Reported as reported.** Testimony is summarised faithfully and attributed to its source code. Figures in testimony are the source's own account, and are marked as such.
6. **Never silently replaced.** An entry is changed only by a reviewed change to this record. A superseded or retired entry stays readable with the reason, following the principle of rule 7 of the Capability Truth and Delivery Policy.
7. **Linked, not duplicated.** Each entry names the existing capability, contract gap or open decision it bears on. An entry never creates a capability.

### SRH-001 — Corporate development funding as a possible financing route

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Class | `STRATEGIC_RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS` |
| Origin | PT-01, who reports that large-company development funding has been discussed as a possible alternative financing route, and that they have **not personally observed an established model** |
| Status | `UNVERIFIED`. Open. |
| Hypothesis | Large companies might fund agricultural development programmes in which AAB participates, as an alternative or complement to government and donor funding. |
| What would need to be true | Companies with a relevant interest exist and would fund such programmes on terms compatible with country sovereignty, institutional data control and scientific independence; the arrangement is lawful in the country concerned; and the funder gains no influence over scientific outputs. |
| Evidence that could test it | Documented cases of corporate-funded agricultural development programmes and their outcomes; legal, tax and governance advice; institutional and country views on acceptability; conflict-of-interest analysis. |
| Bears on | The Commercial domain, which "never decides scientific truth" (domain register, section 3); CAP-21 records a payer, sponsor or subsidy only for an actual agreement (its `paymentBasis` and explicit-subsidy rules); `governance/AAB-COMMERCIAL-PRICING-PRINCIPLES-2026-09-23.md`, "Who pays"; CAP-05's rule that evidence is never weighted by who produced it. |
| What it is not | Not a funding model, not an endorsement of any company, not an available funding opportunity, and not an AAB financing commitment. It creates no CAP-21 record. |

## 6. Where this register sits

This register is a governance record, not part of the capability architecture. It is the place for hypotheses and testimony that are not yet, and may never become, evidence. The Capability Truth and Delivery Policy governs claims about what AAB itself can do; this register holds unverified propositions about the world AAB works in. Neither is a substitute for the other.

The Platform Owner maintains this register. Changes are reviewed and merged under the pull request rule.
