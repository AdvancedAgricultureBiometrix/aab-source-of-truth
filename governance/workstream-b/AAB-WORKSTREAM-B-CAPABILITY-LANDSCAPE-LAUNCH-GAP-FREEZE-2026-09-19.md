## Landscape-wide invariants

### 1. No Automatic State Promotion Across Governed Boundaries

A preceding state never automatically establishes the next governed state.

Examples include:

- hypothesis does not become fact merely because AAB generated it;
- candidate material does not become an approved ingredient;
- formulation generation does not establish validity, efficacy, safety or trial authority;
- trial outcome does not become approved learning;
- dossier completeness does not become regulatory approval;
- manufacturing transfer does not become production authority;
- authentication does not become user authority;
- payment does not become activation;
- technical qualification does not become commissioning;
- an audit with no findings does not establish an untested control as verified;
- simulation state never becomes production state.

### 2. Evidence Belongs to the State That Produced It

Evidence is bound to the material system, scientific object, configuration, version and authority state against which it was established.

A material change may make prior evidence stale and require targeted reassessment.

Release management must therefore support a **Qualification-Impact Declaration** and, where technically possible, automated cross-checking of changed code/configuration against declared impact categories.

Affected evidence must be capable of entering **STALE — REASSESSMENT REQUIRED** rather than remaining silently green.

### 3. Tracks Are Non-Compensating

Tracks may progress in parallel, but cannot give readiness credit to one another.

- a polished simulation does not compensate for incomplete sovereignty;
- commissioned infrastructure does not prove scientific capability;
- commercial entitlement does not establish technical readiness;
- scientific capability does not establish commissioning.

## CAP-14 launch correction

The canonical country-local messaging architecture requires scientific collaboration to remain inside the same sovereign country boundary as the science. The canonical egress specification classifies scientific summaries, formulations, prompts/context, screenshots and support artefacts containing protected science as protected country scientific information.

Therefore AAB must not assume launch scientists can use uncontrolled external communication for protected scientific discussion.

**CAP-14A — Minimum Sovereign Scientific Messaging** is Launch Release.

**CAP-14B — Advanced Collaboration** is Post-Launch.

CAP-14A launch scope is intentionally narrow: authorised country-local messaging, governed threads, persisted recipient authority, basic governed scientific-object references, permitted screenshots where separately controlled, required audit metadata, no cross-country recipients and no external forwarding by default.

## CAP-34 placement

CAP-34 is **PRE-LAUNCH / EVALUATION-ENABLING**, not an operational Launch Release capability.

Its purpose is to let a prospective country experience released AAB behaviour against controlled synthetic/reference scenarios before supplying sovereign scientific information or receiving a production country environment.

**Build priority does not equal commissioning criticality.**

CAP-34 may be a high near-term Workstream-B build priority while contributing zero commissioning credit.

## CAP-34 hard boundaries

CAP-34 must:

- use controlled synthetic/public/reference demonstration information only;
- use actual released AAB capability logic wherever available rather than scripted fake results;
- label concept-only experiences explicitly as **CONCEPT PREVIEW — NOT AN IMPLEMENTED CAPABILITY**;
- visibly identify every simulation surface as non-production;
- prevent simulation identities, memberships, approvals, entitlements, evidence, learning and other state from becoming production state;
- prohibit automatic real-country-to-simulation data flow;
- prohibit simulation-to-production state promotion;
- be resettable to a clean simulation baseline;
- demonstrate blocked, contradictory and insufficient-evidence cases as well as happy paths.

Permanent visible statement:

> **CONTROLLED AAB SIMULATION — SYNTHETIC/REFERENCE DATA ONLY. This environment does not represent AAB's current commissioning status, does not prove readiness for production use of real scientific data, and carries no production, government, scientific, regulatory or commissioning authority.**
