## What AAB is for: the complete operating chain

AAB supports the whole chain by which a country turns a need into lasting capability:

1. **Country need.** Work starts from a need a country has identified, not from a product
   looking for a market.
2. **Evidence discovery and admission.** The evidence relevant to that need is found and
   admitted honestly: attributable, with its provenance, its limitations and its gaps
   recorded.
3. **Governed reasoning.** Admitted evidence is reasoned over under governance: traceable,
   reproducible, and never claiming more than the evidence supports.
4. **Investigation.** Open questions are investigated, and the results are admitted as
   evidence in their own right.
5. **Development and trials.** Candidate solutions are developed and tested, and their results
   are recorded as honestly as any other evidence.
6. **Regulatory translation.** What is known is translated into the form a regulatory
   framework requires, with every gap and every human decision disclosed.
7. **Controlled practical transfer.** What has been proven is transferred into practice under
   control, by accountable people.
8. **Preserved institutional learning.** What was learned, including what failed, is kept as
   governed institutional memory, so the country does not start from nothing next time.

Each stage is a set of capabilities. Each capability states its own maturity (below). This
document asserts no stage's maturity.

## Domains are applications of the platform

AGR (Agricultural Science), SCS (Supply Chain Sovereignty) and future domains are applications
of the platform. None of them defines it.

- **AGR** applies the platform to agricultural science: discovering, admitting and reasoning
  over scientific evidence, and preserving institutional scientific memory.
- **SCS** applies it to supply chain sovereignty: admitting supply chain evidence honestly, and
  evaluating whether it is sufficient under a regulatory framework.
- **Future domains** apply the same platform to other needs.

The shared mechanisms, such as identity and authority, immutable evidence, provenance, frozen
evaluations, attributable human decisions, receipts, and country isolation, belong to the
platform. Each domain supplies its own policy. See
`governance/AAB-PLATFORM-DOMAIN-SEPARATION-DECISION-2026-09-25.md`.

## Five maturity states

Every capability, in every domain, is in exactly one of five maturity states. They are
distinct, and no statement about AAB may blur them:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Designed** | A canonical contract defines the capability. |
| **Implemented** | Code exists that performs what the contract defines. |
| **Behaviourally proven** | Tests or proofs demonstrate the behaviour end to end, honestly, over real inputs. For the SCS pilot this is recorded as `MINIMUM_VERTICAL_SLICE_PROVEN`. |
| **Admitted** | The capability has passed the ten-point admission checklist and is a canonical capability of the platform. |
| **Commissioned** | The capability is authorised for operational use through commissioning. |

Each state requires the one before it. Being in one state never implies the next: a
behaviourally proven capability is not admitted, and an admitted capability is not
commissioned.

As of this record, SCS-CAP-01 to SCS-CAP-06 are behaviourally proven (minimum vertical slice)
in the SCS pilot. No capability in any domain is admitted or commissioned.

## Permanent platform values

These values come from the existing document. They are platform values, not SCS values: they
bind every domain.

**Smallholder inclusion.** The people existing tools exclude are the test of every design
decision. A smallholder with GPS coordinates and no formal land title is not excluded: they are
admitted honestly, their gap is recorded explicitly, and the person accountable is given what
they need to make a real decision. The gap is never papered over, and the smallholder is never
silently excluded. In every domain, the equivalent person, the one the documentation
infrastructure was never designed for, is included honestly rather than excluded.

**Honest gap disclosure.** The platform does not round up and does not look away. Coverage of
87% is not recorded as 100%; the missing 13% is disclosed, with what it means. A conflict
between admitted sources is surfaced as a conflict, not resolved by selecting the more
convenient source. The accountable person sees both sides, decides, and the platform records
their reasoning permanently. A platform that refuses to manufacture sufficiency is harder to
sell than one that produces green checkmarks. It is also the only one worth building.

The discipline behind both values stands for the whole platform: the refusal to build
something that only appears to help people while actually protecting the institutions using
it.
