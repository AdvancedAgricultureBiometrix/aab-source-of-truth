# AAB Platform Purpose and Values — 2026-09-22

**Status:** PLATFORM RECORD — WEBSITE CANDIDATE
**Authority:** RECORDS THE PURPOSE, VALUES AND INTENDED BENEFICIARIES OF THE AAB 
PLATFORM. This document does not admit capabilities, grant implementation authority,
or alter commissioning status.

## Why AAB exists

AAB is not built for the institutions and compliance officers who already have 
adequate tools. It is built for the people and institutions the existing tools 
were never designed to serve.

The compliance officer in Bangkok trying to defend a due diligence statement to 
an EU customs authority. The Vietnamese coffee cooperative trying to stay 
connected to European markets through regulations that assume documentation 
systems they don't have. The Thai rubber smallholder whose livelihood depends 
on being traceable through a supply chain that was never designed with them in 
mind. The Ghanaian cocoa farmer facing the same problem at larger scale, with 
less institutional support, and less time.

Those are the people this platform is built for.

## What this means in practice

Every design decision in AAB is made with those people in mind — not as an 
afterthought, but as the test.

A smallholder farmer with GPS coordinates and no formal land title is not 
excluded from the platform. They are admitted honestly, their gap is recorded 
explicitly, and the compliance officer is given the information they need to 
make a real decision. The gap is never papered over. The farmer is never 
silently excluded.

A satellite image that covers 87% of a registered plot does not become coverage 
of 100%. The 13% that is missing is disclosed, surfaced, and the reviewer is 
told exactly what it means for their due diligence position. The system does not 
round up. It does not look away.

A conflict between two admitted evidence sources — a satellite analysis and a 
forestry authority record that reach different conclusions about the same plot 
in the same period — is surfaced as a conflict, not resolved by selecting the 
more convenient source. The reviewer sees both. The reviewer decides. The 
platform records their reasoning permanently.

These are not technical constraints. They are values expressed through 
architecture.

## The discipline behind the ambition

The ambition is to help ordinary people in extraordinary circumstances — 
smallholder farmers, rural cooperatives, compliance officers in under-resourced 
institutions, government agencies in countries where the digital infrastructure 
that wealthier nations take for granted does not yet exist.

The discipline is the refusal to build something that only appears to help them 
while actually protecting the institutions using it.

A platform that produces honest gap disclosures and refuses to manufacture 
sufficiency is harder to sell than one that produces green checkmarks. It is 
also the only one worth building if the goal is genuine help rather than the 
appearance of it.

## Who this platform serves

**Smallholder farmers and rural communities** — whose land, labour, and 
knowledge are the foundation of global commodity supply chains, and who are 
most at risk of being excluded from markets by regulations designed without 
them in mind.

**Cooperatives and aggregators** — who manage evidence collection across 
hundreds or thousands of smallholder suppliers and need tools that handle the 
complexity of informal land tenure, fragmented documentation, and inconsistent 
GPS coverage honestly rather than by exclusion.

**Compliance officers and trade documentation specialists** — who bear legal 
responsibility for due diligence statements and need a system that tells them 
exactly what they know, what they don't, and what the difference means — not 
one that produces false confidence.

**National institutions and government agencies** — in countries where the 
capacity to support smallholder supply chain compliance is limited, and where 
the platform can provide the governed intelligence layer that institutions need 
to engage with international regulatory requirements on honest terms.

**Countries preparing for what comes next** — because EUDR is not the last 
regulation of its kind. Carbon border adjustment, conflict minerals, modern 
slavery supply chains, food safety traceability — the same structural problem 
will arrive in different forms. Countries and institutions that build governed 
evidence infrastructure now are prepared for what is coming. Those that don't 
will face each new regulation from the same unprepared position.

## The world this platform is built for

The world is changing faster than the institutions designed to govern it. 
Regulations that assume documentation systems, digital infrastructure, and 
institutional capacity that do not exist in most commodity-producing countries 
are being imposed on supply chains built on informal relationships, traditional 
land rights, and generations of knowledge that was never written down.

AAB does not pretend that problem away. It builds the governed intelligence 
layer that allows real evidence — with all its gaps, all its uncertainty, and 
all its honest complexity — to be admitted, evaluated, and acted on by the 
people who are accountable for the decisions.

That is what the platform is for. That is why every design decision is made 
the way it is. And that is the standard against which every future decision 
will be held.

## Note on website use

This document is a candidate for inclusion on the aab.ag platform website. 
Its language is intentionally accessible — written for institutions, 
cooperatives, government agencies, and individuals who need to understand 
what AAB is and why it exists, not only for technical reviewers of the 
platform architecture.

When adapted for the website, the specific examples — Thai rubber smallholders, 
Vietnamese coffee cooperatives, Ghanaian cocoa farmers — should be retained. 
They are not generic. They are the specific people and situations that the 
platform design has been tested against, and they communicate the platform's 
genuine scope and intent more accurately than abstract statements of purpose.
